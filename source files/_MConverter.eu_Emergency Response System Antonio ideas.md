**Emergency Response System -- Micromax Pty Ltd**

Goldstream RV

75 Bald Hill Rd Pakenham VIC 3180

Contact: Russell Willmott

Goldstream RV is an Australian RV manufacturer operating for 30+ years.

The factory is a single level U-shaped building around a central open storage area.

Building construction is mostly steel sheet over steel frame work. There are several internal steel sheet walls.

Scenario 1

Some factory staff can operate independently of others inside an RV for hours at a time. If they have a medical emergency, then it could be sometime before they are discovered. This might not be until end-of-day closure when their car is discovered still in the carpark. If they have a car...

Scenario 2

In case of a fire or other emergency evacuation, the location of independent staff is not known at all times. If a missing staff member does not hear the sirens or is incapacitated, then a full search is required putting further staff and management at risk.

Scenario 3

At end-of-day closure, if it is assumed that the bundy clock sign-off has been forgotten, then the gates may be locked with staff still inside the building.

Scenario 4

Sign-in via the bundy clock has been forgotten by the staff member, so management do not know if the staff member is on site. Regular sub-contractors do not use the bundy system.

Scenario 5

Some staff work at irregular hours, sometimes being the only one in either part of or the whole of the factory. A medical emergency i.e. a collapse, will not be noticed.

The current bundy clock system sends its data for remote payroll processing. An overtime report is sent to the Production Manager for approval.
